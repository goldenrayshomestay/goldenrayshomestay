About Golden Rays

A quiet coastal homestay, close to everything the beach has to offer

Welcome to Golden Rays Beach Stay, a comfortable coastal getaway where you can enjoy peaceful surroundings and easy access to the beach. Our property offers comfortable rooms, private spaces and balconies where guests can relax and enjoy the surrounding views.

Located just around 100 metres from the shore, it is an ideal place for a refreshing beach escape — away from the crowds, but never far from the sand.

Comfortable rooms
Non-AC and AC rooms with double beds and private bathrooms.
Private balconies
Step out to green, open views right from your room.
Peaceful surroundings
Set among coconut palms on Bengre Main Road, Mangaluru.
